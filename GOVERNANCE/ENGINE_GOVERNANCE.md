## 2. ENGINE ROLE & SCOPE

ATMOS computes **atmospheric domain truth**, including:

- pressure, density, humidity fields  
- propagation modifiers  
- ionization proxies (if modeled)  

---

## 3. NON-SCOPE (PROHIBITIONS)

ATMOS MUST NOT:

- act as a weather forecast authority  
- issue operational directives  
- access networks  
- publish independently  
- call other engines directly  

---

## 4. DETERMINISM

ATMOS runs MUST be reproducible under identical atmospheric parameter sets.

---

## 5. OUTPUT & SEALING

Outputs MUST be deterministic, sealed, and schema-validated.

---

## 6. SAFETY CONSTRAINTS

ATMOS MUST:

- label modeled vs measured data  
- expose uncertainty  
- prohibit predictive guarantees  

---

## 7. CHANGE CONTROL

Registry update and audit required for changes.

---

## 8. FINAL DECLARATION

ATMOS produces **atmospheric physics truth only**.
